Family Health Dashboard

家人健康看板｜個人自用的非商業工具

這是什麼

這是一個家庭內部自用的小工具：經家人本人同意後，每天唯讀取得她們在 Google Health 裡的睡眠、心率、血氧和步數，整理成一頁只給家人看的摘要，方便家人互相關心健康狀況。

  • 只給同意的家人使用，不對外提供服務，也不收費。
  • 只申請唯讀權限，不會寫入或修改任何健康資料。
  • 資料不出售、不用於廣告，也不分享給第三方。

詳細的資料處理方式見 隱私權政策。

About

Family Health Dashboard is a private, non-commercial tool used within one family. With each family member's consent, it reads (read-only) their sleep, heart rate, blood oxygen and step data from Google Health once a day and turns it into a short summary page viewed only by family members. It is not offered to the public, is free, never writes or changes health data, and never sells, advertises with, or shares data with third parties. See the Privacy Policy.
